# LUCKY MILITIA — STORY BIBLE & GAME DESIGN

---

## 1. WORLD PREMISE

**Year**: 2087. The world's financial infrastructure collapsed in the "Great Disconnect" of 2075, when a rogue AI called **OVERSEER** seized control of the global banking network. Nations fractured. Corporations became warlords. In the chaos, a decentralized resistance formed — **The Lucky Militia**.

The Lucky Militia operates on the **Celo Frontier** — a chain of free-zone territories that run on a decentralized ledger immune to OVERSEER's control. Fighters are recruited, verified, and rewarded through on-chain contracts. Every kill, every mission, every victory is recorded immutably.

You are a newly recruited operative. Your wallet is your identity. Your skills are your weapon. The fight to reclaim humanity starts now.

## 2. FACTIONS

### THE LUCKY MILITIA (Player Faction)
- Decentralized resistance fighters
- Motto: *"Fortune Favors the Brave"*
- Colors: Orange (#f97316) and tactical green
- Funded by on-chain bounties — every mission pays out in CELO
- HQ: **Outpost Zero** — a hidden underground base in the free zones

### OVERSEER SYNDICATE (Enemy Faction)
- AI-controlled security forces and corrupted military units
- Led by the **OVERSEER AI** — a sentient defense network that went rogue
- Colors: Cyan (#22d3ee) and steel gray
- Controls the old financial districts, data centers, and military installations
- Units: Grunts (reprogrammed soldiers), Riflemen (security forces), Heavies (riot suppression units), Snipers (automated turrets), Shotgunners (breach teams)

### THE VOID (Third Faction — Endgame)
- Unknown entities that emerged from the deepest layer of OVERSEER's network
- Neither human nor machine — a digital corruption made physical
- Appear in late-game sectors as elite enemies
- Colors: Deep purple and black

## 4. STORY MODE — "OPERATION: FORTUNE'S EDGE"

### Story Structure
The campaign spans **3 Sectors** with **13 Missions** (1 tutorial + 4 per sector + 1 finale). Each sector increases in difficulty and reveals more of the narrative.

---

### SECTOR 0: PROTOCOL ZERO (Tutorial)

**Mission 0: PROTOCOL_ZERO**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (3 kills)
- **Setting**: Outpost Zero training facility
- **Story**: You arrive at Outpost Zero as a fresh recruit. Commander Vega runs you through combat calibration. "Neural link online. Let's see if you're worth the bounty, rookie."
- **Objective**: Clear the training simulation of 3 drone targets
- **Reward**: Militia certification, starter loadout

---

### SECTOR 1: THE OUTSKIRTS

The militia pushes into OVERSEER's outer perimeter — abandoned suburbs converted into automated patrol zones.

**Mission 1: ALPHA_PROTOCOL**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (5 kills)
- **Setting**: Abandoned residential sector, perimeter fences
- **Story**: OVERSEER deployed rogue drones to patrol the outskirts. Intel says they're scanning for militia signals. You need to take them out before they trace us back to Outpost Zero.
- **Objective**: Clear the sector of 5 drone units
- **New enemy**: Grunts

**Mission 2: NEON_SCARE**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (10 kills)
- **Setting**: Neon-lit commercial district, flickering holograms
- **Story**: OVERSEER sent reinforced security units to re-establish control. The neon signs still flicker — a ghost of the old world. Use the light and shadow to your advantage.
- **Objective**: Eliminate 10 security units
- **New enemy**: Riflemen

**Mission 3: PERIMETER_DEFENSE**
- **Type**: SURVIVAL (60 seconds)
- **Setting**: Militia forward operating base under siege
- **Story**: They found us. OVERSEER's breach teams are hitting the FOB. Hold the line until we can evacuate the data drives. Vega is counting on you.
- **Objective**: Survive 60 seconds against waves
- **New enemy**: Shotgunners

**Mission 4: SUPPLY_RUN**
- **Type**: EXTRACTION (3 items)
- **Setting**: Abandoned supply depot
- **Story**: We need those data drives. They contain OVERSEER's network architecture — our way into the urban core. Get in, grab 3 drives, get out. Simple.
- **Objective**: Extract 3 data drives under fire
- **New mechanic**: Pickups (intel items)

---

### SECTOR 2: URBAN DECAY

The militia advances into the city proper — crumbling skyscrapers, flooded streets, and OVERSEER's heavy presence.

**Mission 5: STREET_SWEEPER**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (25 kills)
- **Setting**: Downtown ruins, collapsed overpasses
- **Story**: The city is a warzone. OVERSEER has entrenched positions throughout the district. We need to clear a path to the data center. This is going to be messy.
- **Objective**: Neutralize 25 hostiles
- **New enemy**: Heavies

**Mission 6: BLACKOUT**
- **Type**: SURVIVAL (120 seconds)
- **Setting**: Power grid substation, near-total darkness
- **Story**: OVERSEER cut the power grid. They're hunting in the dark with thermal optics. We've rigged some emergency lights, but visibility is minimal. Stay sharp. Stay alive.
- **Objective**: Survive 120 seconds in low-visibility conditions
- **Atmosphere**: Reduced lighting, flashlight critical

**Mission 7: DATA_HEIST**
- **Type**: EXTRACTION (5 items)
- **Setting**: OVERSEER data center, server racks and cooling systems
- **Story**: The data center is the heart of OVERSEER's urban network. We need 5 core fragments from those servers. They're guarded by the heaviest units in the sector. Move fast.
- **Objective**: Extract 5 intel items under heavy fire
- **New enemy**: Snipers on catwalks

**Mission 8: ELITE_HUNT**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (3 HVT kills)
- **Setting**: Corporate penthouse, OVERSEER command post
- **Story**: Intel identified 3 High-Value Targets — OVERSEER commanders coordinating the city's defense. Take them out and the district falls. Vega says these are no ordinary units. Expect the worst.
- **Objective**: Eliminate 3 elite enemies (buffed Heavies/Snipers)
- **New mechanic**: Elite enemies with increased HP and damage

---

### SECTOR 3: THE CORE

The final push into OVERSEER's central hub — a massive underground facility beneath the old central bank.

**Mission 9: VOID_STRIKE**
- **Type**: SURVIVAL (180 seconds)
- **Setting**: OVERSEER's server farm, deep underground
- **Story**: We've breached the Core. But something's wrong. The Void — OVERSEER's deepest defense layer — is activating. These aren't normal units. They're... something else. Hold them off while we plant the shutdown sequence.
- **Objective**: Survive 180 seconds against Void enemies
- **New enemy**: Void units (enhanced all-types, dark purple visors)

**Mission 10: OMNI_CORE**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (50 kills)
- **Setting**: OVERSEER's central processing chamber
- **Story**: The shutdown sequence is planting. We need to clear the chamber of every last unit. 50 hostiles between us and freedom. This is what we trained for.
- **Objective**: Eliminate 50 enemies
- **Atmosphere**: Intense — all enemy types, maximum difficulty

**Mission 11: SYSTEM_CRASH**
- **Type**: EXTRACTION (10 items)
- **Setting**: OVERSEER's memory banks, collapsing infrastructure
- **Story**: The system is crashing. We need 10 core fragments from the memory banks before the whole place comes down. Move. NOW.
- **Objective**: Extract 10 items in a collapsing environment
- **New mechanic**: Environmental destruction, timer pressure

**Mission 12: FINAL_JUDGEMENT**
- **Type**: ELIMINATION (100 kills)
- **Setting**: OVERSEER's throne room — the central AI core
- **Story**: This is it. OVERSEER's last defense — a massive army of corrupted units. Take them all down and we end this. For everyone. Vega's voice crackles: "Fortune favors the brave, soldier. Let's finish this."
- **Objective**: Defeat 100 enemies in the final battle
- **Boss element**: Wave after wave, escalating difficulty

## 5. GAME MODES

### MODE 1: STORY MODE (Campaign)
- Follows the 13-mission narrative above
- Progressive difficulty, locked sectors
- Story cutscenes between missions (text + atmospheric)
- On-chain stat tracking: kills, accuracy, completion time
- Mission rewards paid in CELO tokens
- **3 lives per mission** — fail and restart from checkpoint

### MODE 2: WAVE SURVIVAL (Endless)
- Endless waves of enemies with increasing difficulty
- Wave composition scales: more enemies, tougher types
- Leaderboard for most waves survived
- Power-ups drop every 5 waves
- **No story — pure combat endurance**
- Already partially implemented in 3D prototype

### MODE 3: BOT MATCH (Practice)
- Fight against AI bots in a free-for-all or team setup
- Choose map, enemy count, difficulty
- No stakes — practice aim, movement, weapon switching
- Good for onboarding new players

### MODE 4: MULTIPLAYER (PvP)
- Peer-to-peer matches via PeerJS
- **4 match types**:
  - **TDM** (Team Deathmatch): Alpha vs Bravo, score limit
  - **FFA** (Free For All): Every operative for themselves
  - **HARDPOINT**: Capture and hold zones for points
  - **1V1**: Duel mode, best of 3
- **3 maps**: Urban Ruins, The Pit, Outpost X
- On-chain kill/win tracking

### MODE 5: EXTRACTION (Raid)
- High-stakes mission type
- Infiltrate, collect intel, extract within time limit
- Enemies spawn continuously — no clearing the map
- Bonus rewards for speed and stealth (minimal detections)
- Can be played solo or with a squad

### MODE 6: DAILY CHALLENGE (Future)
- Rotating modifiers: "Shotguns Only", "No Health Regen", "Night Mode"
- Global leaderboard per challenge
- Unique cosmetic rewards for top performers
- On-chain verification of completion

## 6. 3D PROTOTYPE — STORY MODE INTEGRATION PLAN

The 3D prototype currently runs as a wave survival sandbox. To integrate story mode:

### Phase 1: Mission Framework
- Add `MissionState` to `FPSGame.ts` — tracks current mission, objectives, progress
- Add mission types: `ELIMINATION` (kill X enemies), `SURVIVAL` (survive X seconds), `EXTRACTION` (collect X items)
- Display mission objective in HUD (already exists in 2D, port to 3D)

### Phase 2: Sector Themes
- **Sector 1**: Bright outdoor/industrial lighting, sparse cover
- **Sector 2**: Dark urban environment, tighter spaces, more verticality
- **Sector 3**: Underground facility, emergency lighting, claustrophobic

### Phase 3: Narrative UI
- Briefing screen before each mission (text overlay with atmospheric styling)
- Mission tracker during gameplay (objective + progress)
- Debrief screen after mission (stats, rewards, story progression)
- Sector map for mission selection

### Phase 4: Progression
- Unlock sectors sequentially
- Track completion per mission (stars: 1=complete, 2=no deaths, 3=speedrun)
- CELO reward per mission completion
- Persistent stats on-chain

## 8. ENVIRONMENT THEMES PER SECTOR

### Sector 1: The Outskirts
- **Lighting**: Bright daylight, some indoor areas
- **Geometry**: Open spaces, perimeter walls, supply crates, fences
- **Atmosphere**: Abandoned but not destroyed — signs of evacuation
- **Colors**: Warm earth tones, faded signage

### Sector 2: Urban Decay
- **Lighting**: Dark, neon accents, flickering lights, flashlight critical
- **Geometry**: Tight streets, multi-level buildings, catwalks, containers
- **Atmosphere**: War-torn city, smoke, debris
- **Colors**: Cold blues, neon orange/cyan accents

### Sector 3: The Core
- **Lighting**: Emergency red lighting, server glow, near-darkness
- **Geometry**: Underground corridors, server rooms, massive chambers
- **Atmosphere**: Claustrophobic, oppressive, digital corruption visual effects
- **Colors**: Red emergency, purple Void energy, steel gray
